# Resume Bullet Points

If you are applying for a Data Analyst, BI Analyst, or Power BI Developer role, you can add these bullet points under your "Projects" or "Experience" section on your resume.

### Option 1 (Focus on End-to-End BI Development)
* **Supply Chain Analytics Dashboard:** Transformed a flat operational dataset into a multi-dimensional Star Schema using Power Query, developing reusable DAX measures to analyze revenue, supplier reliability, and logistics efficiency.
* Designed an interactive 5-page Power BI dashboard with executive KPI cards, drill-down capabilities, and scatter plot analyses to identify high-risk inventory stockouts and supplier defect trends.

### Option 2 (Focus on Data Modeling and DAX)
* Architected a Power BI data model by branching a single-table supply chain dataset into a robust Star Schema (Fact and 4 Dimension tables), significantly improving report performance and cross-filtering accuracy.
* Authored complex DAX measures replacing implicit aggregations to track Total Revenue, Average Lead Time, Shipping Costs, and a dynamic Stock-to-Demand ratio metric.

### Option 3 (Focus on Business Impact & Insights)
* Developed an enterprise-grade Supply Chain Analytics dashboard in Power BI, providing stakeholders with clear visibility into product profitability, logistics bottlenecks, and supplier manufacturing costs.
* Established an inventory risk-flagging system using DAX to identify overstocked vs. understocked SKUs, improving data-driven decision-making for supply chain operations.
